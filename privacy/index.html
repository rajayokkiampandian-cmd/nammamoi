This policy explains what Namma MOI handles, why it is used, where it is processed/stored, and how you can delete it.

Our commitment

We do not sell personal data. Namma MOI uses data only to provide, secure, support and improve the service. Contact: support@nammamoi.in.

2. Storage and service providers

Namma MOI uses Google services including Google OAuth, Apps Script, Sheets and Drive for authenticated application storage and processing. When Receipt OCR is used, receipt image content may be sent to Google Cloud Vision for text extraction. A Drive-based OCR fallback may be used when the primary receipt OCR path is unavailable.

Local Android storage may keep limited app/session state and a structured record cache for faster use. Attachments and Documents remain in Google Drive-backed storage rather than being permanently duplicated into the app cache.

3. Ask assistant and public information

The Ask assistant is primarily a deterministic/local app intent router. For a general-knowledge question that does not match private app data, it may query Wikipedia's public API and return a source link. Questions containing personal identifiers/secrets or clearly private app-data requests are blocked from being sent to the public lookup path.

4. Permissions and device access

Camera/gallery/file access is used only when you choose capture or upload actions. SMS import uses user-initiated Share/file import; the launch build does not require READ_SMS or RECEIVE_SMS. Notifications are used for reminders when you allow them. Biometric access, when enabled, is used for local app-lock convenience.

5. Family / Sub-user access

Family data may be accessible to Sub Users explicitly added by the Main User, subject to Viewer / Editor / Admin permissions. Namma MOI separates family workspaces and enforces access checks on the server as well as in the UI.

6. Retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated app-managed data from My Account → Privacy & Account → Delete my account & data. You can also use the public deletion page after uninstalling the app.

Main-account deletion removes the family app account, linked Sub-user accounts, app-managed family Sheets, Documents metadata/files, app-managed attachments/backups, linked feedback/payment-request rows and authentication/session state. Sub-user deletion removes that login/profile while preserving shared family financial history; retained attribution is replaced with a non-identifying “Deleted user” label.

Google/platform infrastructure may temporarily retain limited technical/security logs or trashed-file recovery copies according to Google service retention behavior. Short-lived Apps Script/OCR caches may remain only until configured expiry and are not used for normal account access after deletion. If a Drive-permission revoke temporarily fails, the minimum account email and Drive object identifier needed to retry that security cleanup may be retained only until the revoke succeeds; completed retry history is de-identified.

7. Security

We use authenticated Google identity, App PIN access controls, tenant separation, role-based permissions and server-side validation. No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your Google account and Namma MOI App PIN.

8. Children

Namma MOI is a household record-management application and is not designed as a service directed to children. Do not create an account for a child who cannot legally consent to the handling of their data.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
