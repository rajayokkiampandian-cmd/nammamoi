How deletion works

  1. Enter the registered Namma MOI email.
  2. A 6-digit deletion OTP is sent only if the account exists.
  3. Enter the OTP and confirm permanent deletion.
  4. Server-side cleanup removes/de-identifies the applicable Namma MOI account data.

What is deleted?

Main-account deletion covers the Namma MOI family account, linked Sub-user accounts, app-managed Sheets, Documents metadata/files, app-managed attachments/backups, linked feedback/payment-request rows and authentication/session state. Sub-user deletion removes the user's login/profile and de-identifies personal attribution retained in shared family records.

Some Google/platform security logs, trashed-file recovery copies or short-lived service caches can remain temporarily under provider retention behavior. See the Privacy Policy for details.
